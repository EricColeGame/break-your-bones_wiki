Most Roblox games ask you to survive. [Break your Bones](https://www.roblox.com/games/123821081589134/Break-your-Bones) asks you to fail as spectacularly as possible. The premise is right there in the name: you fling a ragdoll character off ledges, into hazards, and down huge drops, and the goal is to break every bone in the body. It is a physics sandbox first and a challenge second, which is why it has quietly racked up about 133 million visits since its June 2025 launch.

This guide sticks to what the developer actually documents, separates that from what players report, and is honest about the parts nobody has confirmed.

## What Break your Bones Actually Is

Break your Bones is a Roblox experience created by the group **High Effort Projects**. According to the official Roblox page, the place was created on June 5, 2025, and was last updated on October 13, 2026. Servers support up to 20 players at a time.

The official description is short and specific. It advertises:

- Trying to break all of your bones
- A fun bone-smashing simulator
- Unlocking new ragdolls
- Flinging yourself from a huge drop
- A ragdoll physics simulation

That is the entire official feature list. Notably absent: any mention of codes, currencies, shops, prices, damage numbers, or a ragdoll catalog. If you see a guide claiming to know the exact damage value of a specific fall or the price of an unlock, treat it as unverified. The developer has not published those details.

It is also worth being clear about what this game is *not*. It is not **Broken Bones IV**, a separate Roblox experience with different mechanics. It is also not any of the mobile bone-breaking apps on Google Play or the App Store, which share similar wording but are unrelated products from different developers. If you searched for one of those and landed here, the Roblox version is its own thing.

## How the Core Loop Works

The loop is simple by design. You spawn as a ragdoll, you find height or a hazard, and you let physics do the rest. The official description emphasizes two things: breaking all your bones and doing it from a huge drop. Everything else in the game exists to support that.

In practice, this means the fun comes from experimentation rather than from following a strict progression path. You are not grinding for a meta build. You are testing what happens when a ragdoll hits a surface at a given angle and speed. Because the game runs on Roblox's physics engine, results can vary between attempts, which is part of the appeal for players who enjoy sandbox chaos.

The 20-player server cap matters here. With a full server, you get a crowd of ragdolls doing ridiculous things at once, which turns a solo physics toy into a shared spectacle. That social element is a big reason the game has sustained its visit count.

## Breaking Bones: What Is and Is Not Documented

This is where most third-party guides go wrong, so it is worth being precise.

If you are looking for Break your Bones codes, the honest answer is that none are documented by the developer. That does not mean codes will never exist, but nobody can hand you a working list today without inventing it.

If you want to check for yourself, do two things. First, open the official Roblox page and read the description and any developer notes there. Second, look for a High Effort Projects community space, such as a group page or an associated Discord, where the developer would announce anything official. Those are the only sources that can create a real code.

## Practical Tips for New Players

Because the developer publishes no stat tables, the best advice is about approach rather than numbers.

**Start with height, not hazards.** The "huge drop" is the feature the description calls out, so it is the natural first experiment. Learn how your ragdoll behaves in free fall before adding obstacles.

**Watch the angles.** Ragdoll physics is unpredictable by nature. A fall that breaks nothing on one attempt can behave differently on the next. Adjust your launch direction and see what changes.

**Play with a fuller server.** The 20-player cap means a busy server gives you more chaos and more ideas to copy. Watching other players' approaches is the fastest way to learn undocumented tricks.

**Do not chase numbers.** If a guide tells you a specific damage value, verify it in-game yourself. Undocumented figures are guesses at best and outdated at worst.

## FAQ

No. They are separate Roblox experiences with different creators and mechanics. Do not assume one game's features apply to the other.

**Does Break your Bones have codes?**
None are documented on the official Roblox page. To check for future codes, monitor the official game page and any High Effort Projects community channel.

**How many players fit in a server?**
Up to 20, per the official page.

**Do I need to play with others?**
No, but a fuller server makes the physics sandbox more entertaining and gives you more ideas to try.

Not from official sources. The description confirms ragdoll unlocking exists but lists no names, prices, or unlock conditions. Verify in-game.
