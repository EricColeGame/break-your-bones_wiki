### Staying Safe While You Break Your Bones Free

Anything popular enough to have a fanbase attracts scammers, and free-to-play physics games are no exception.

- **No generator sites exist.** Any page promising unlimited free currency is harvesting your login.
- **Never share your password** with a "helper" or a Discord bot claiming to grant unlocks.
- **Use the official store only** when buying anything, even a cosmetic.
- **Report clone games** that copy the title and thumbnail of the real one.
- **Set up parental controls** on the host platform if a younger player is involved.

If a deal sounds too generous for a game you can already play for free, it is almost certainly a trap.
