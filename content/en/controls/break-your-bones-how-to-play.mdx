If you have seen short clips of a blocky character tumbling down an enormous staircase and folding in ways that look genuinely uncomfortable, you have probably already watched someone play [Break your Bones](https://www.roblox.com/games/123821081589134/Break-your-Bones). It is a physics ragdoll sandbox on Roblox where the goal is simple and a little bit absurd: fling a character into hazards and long drops, watch the body react to the simulation, and try to break every bone you can.

This article sticks to what the developer actually advertises, separates that from what players report, and is honest about what is simply not documented anywhere official.

## What the official Roblox page actually says

Break your Bones is a Roblox experience created by the group **High Effort Projects**. The official place record shows it was created on **June 5, 2025**, and last updated on **October 13, 2026**. It supports up to **20 players per server** and has accumulated roughly **133 million visits** according to the official record retrieved on October 14, 2026.

The official description advertises a short, clear set of features:

- Try to break all of your bones
- A fun bone-smashing simulator
- Unlock new ragdolls
- Fling yourself from a huge drop
- A ragdoll physics simulation

That is the authoritative list. It is worth noting what is *not* in it: no code system, no currency names, no shop prices, no damage values, no bone-by-bone mechanics, and no ragdoll list. If you see a guide claiming detailed damage numbers or a full roster of unlockable characters, treat it as a third-party observation or an outright guess rather than developer documentation.

One common confusion: this is **not** Broken Bones IV, and it is **not** one of the mobile bone-breaking apps on app stores with similar names. They are separate products with separate mechanics. Anything you read about those games does not transfer here.

## How the sandbox loop works

Based on the advertised description, the core loop is straightforward. You spawn as a ragdoll-driven character, you launch yourself into something dangerous or drop from a great height, and the physics simulation does the rest. The "goal" of breaking all your bones gives the chaos a scoreboard-like purpose.

The reason this format works as a Roblox experience rather than a traditional game is that ragdoll physics produces unpredictable results. The same drop from the same platform will not fold a character identically twice. That variability is the entertainment: players share clips of unusually dramatic impacts, funny mid-air poses, and the occasional perfectly timed fling.

Because the official page does not document a progression system, currency, or shop, you should not assume one exists in a specific form. What the page *does* confirm is that **unlocking new ragdolls** is part of the experience. How you unlock them — playtime, achievements, in-experience currency, or something else — is not stated officially and should be verified in-game.

## Practical tips for getting more out of it

Since the developer has not published a mechanics guide, the most useful advice is about approach rather than hidden numbers.

**Read the environment before you launch.** Physics sandboxes reward experimentation. If a drop looks enormous, it probably is. If a hazard looks like it will send you sideways rather than down, it probably will. Observation beats guesswork here.

**Try different launch angles.** A ragdoll's path and the way its joints react depend heavily on initial velocity and rotation. Small changes in how you enter a drop or a hazard can produce very different results, which is useful if you are chasing specific bone-break outcomes.

**Play with others when you can.** The servers hold up to 20 players, so there is usually company. Watching other players' attempts is often faster than figuring out a hazard yourself, and multiplayer physics interactions can add their own chaos.

**Check the official page and the group's community.** The description is the only developer-authored summary available, and it is short on specifics. If unlock conditions change or new content arrives, the place page's update history and the group's own channels are where it would appear first.
