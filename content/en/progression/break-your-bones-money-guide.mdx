## Why the Physics Matter More Than the Goal

"Ragdoll physics simulation" is not filler text in the description. It is the reason the game feels different from a scripted destruction toy. In a physics-driven sandbox, the character reacts to momentum, gravity, and contact, which means the same hazard can produce different results depending on angle, speed, and timing.

That has real consequences for how you play:

- Height and speed matter. A drop from a huge height is not just "more damage" in the abstract; the ragdoll's velocity at impact changes the collision.
- Angle matters. Landing flat is not the same as landing on an arm or a head.
- Timing matters. Launching yourself at the wrong moment in a hazard cycle changes where and how you land.

Because the game runs on physics rather than fixed damage numbers, there is no reliable way for an outside article to promise a specific result from a specific jump. Anyone who gives you a precise "do this for exactly this outcome" formula is describing their own session, not the game's rules.

## Hazards, Drops, and How to Experiment Productively

The official description points at hazards and one headline feature: flinging yourself from a huge drop. Beyond that, it is quiet on specifics. It does not document a map list, hotkeys, controls, or patch notes.

If you want to improve quickly without guessing blindly, a few habits help:

1. Vary one thing at a time. Same hazard, different approach speed. Same drop height, different body orientation.
2. Watch other players. Servers hold up to 20 people, so you will usually have company. Watching how someone else approaches a hazard is faster than reading about it.
3. Note what unlocks when. Since the official page does not document unlock conditions, keep your own mental or written log. That is more reliable than a third-party list of unknown age.
4. Re-check after updates. The place was last updated October 13, 2026, which means it is being worked on. Behavior you learned last month may not hold this month.

None of this guarantees you will break every bone on your first try. It just gives you a method instead of random flinging.
