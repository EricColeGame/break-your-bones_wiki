## How a Physics Ragdoll Sandbox Like This Works

If you have not played a ragdoll sandbox before, the loop is straightforward to understand even without documented numbers.

You control a character that behaves like a limp body rather than a stiff avatar. You then put that body into situations where physics resolves the outcome: a long fall, a collision with a hazard, a launch at speed. The simulation calculates how the body reacts, and the result is the feedback — crunches, flops, and the sense that you have genuinely wrecked something.

Break your Bones wraps a goal around that loop: break everything. The official description calls the objective "try to break all of your bones," which frames the game as a completion challenge layered on top of a toy. The "huge drop" is the most clearly advertised method of doing damage, and unlocking new ragdolls is the advertised reason to keep going.
