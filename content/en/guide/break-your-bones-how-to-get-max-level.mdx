## How a physics ragdoll sandbox actually plays

Based on the advertised features, the loop is straightforward. You spawn, you find a height or a hazard, you commit to the drop or the launch, and you watch a jointed physics body react. The outcome is emergent rather than scripted: the same fall from the same ledge can produce different tumbles because ragdoll simulation does not replay identically.

A few practical expectations follow from that:
