## The core loop: controlled destruction

The premise is a physics sandbox, not a progression RPG. You launch a character into hazards and large drops, then watch the ragdoll simulation resolve the collision. The "break all your bones" framing is the scoring idea: the more of the skeleton you damage in a run, the more complete the smash.

Because it is a ragdoll simulation, the outcomes are physics-driven rather than scripted. Two identical launches can produce different results depending on angle, speed, and where the body contacts the environment. That variance is the appeal for players who enjoy sandbox chaos, and it is also why precise damage numbers are hard to pin down even from observation.
