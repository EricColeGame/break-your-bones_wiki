If you have seen the phrase "break your bones" attached to a phone game about snapping skeletons, you may be picturing the wrong thing. On Roblox, **Break your Bones** is a physics ragdoll sandbox from the group High Effort Projects, and it plays by very different rules than the mobile puzzle games that share similar words. This guide walks through what the experience actually is, what the developer advertises, what players report seeing in servers, and — just as importantly — what nobody has documented yet.

## What Break your Bones Actually Is

The official Roblox record for place 123821081589134 lists the creator as the High Effort Projects group, with the place created on June 5, 2025, and last updated on October 13, 2026. Servers support up to 20 players, and the experience has accumulated roughly 133 million visits. Those numbers matter because they tell you this is not a small experimental project — it has a large, active audience, which in turn means the live game may contain more than the store page describes.

The official description is short and consistent. It advertises that you try to break all of your bones, calls itself a fun bone-smashing simulator, mentions unlocking new ragdolls, flinging yourself from a huge drop, and describes the whole thing as a ragdoll physics simulation. That is the entire advertised feature set. There is no mention of currency, shops, prices, damage values, codes, or hotkeys.

This distinction is worth internalizing before you read anything else about the game. Roblox experience pages often serve as a storefront blurb rather than a manual, and Break your Bones follows that pattern.

## The Core Loop: Physics, Drops, and Bone Counting

Based on the advertised description, the loop is straightforward. You control a ragdoll, subject it to hazards and dramatic falls, and watch the physics simulation resolve what happens to the body. The "bone" element is the scoring hook: rather than a health bar, the game frames your progress in terms of how many bones you have broken.

The advertised "huge drop" is the signature setup. Launching a ragdoll from a significant height and letting the simulation handle the collision is the clearest expression of what the developer wants the game to be — a toy, not a challenge run. The ragdoll physics simulation is the engine of the fun; the bone count is the feedback.

Because the official page does not publish damage values, fall heights, or a list of hazards, any specific numbers you see elsewhere should be treated as third-party observations rather than developer documentation. If you want to know how a particular drop behaves, the reliable method is to test it in a live server.

## Multiplayer, Server Size, and Why It Matters

At up to 20 players per server, Break your Bones is built for shared chaos rather than solo precision. That design choice shapes the experience in practical ways. Physics simulations with many ragdolls in one place produce unpredictable interactions, and other players become part of the environment whether you intend it or not.

For a new player, joining a full server is often the fastest way to learn what the game actually contains. Watch what other ragdolls are doing, where they are launching from, and what the results look like. The official description gives you the premise; a live server gives you the rest.

Start by reading the official page and accepting its scope as the baseline. ## FAQ

No. They are separate Roblox experiences with different creators. Do not carry mechanics, items, or progression assumptions from one to the other.

**Is it the same as the mobile bone-breaking games?**
No. Break Bones: Fall Challenge and similar Google Play titles are unrelated products. Break your Bones is a Roblox ragdoll physics sandbox.

**Are there working codes?**
None are documented on the official page. To check for yourself, visit the [official experience page](https://www.roblox.com/games/123821081589134/Break-your-Bones) and the High Effort Projects group, and watch for developer announcements there.

**How do I unlock ragdolls?**
The official description confirms ragdoll unlocking exists but does not explain the method. Verify it in-game.

**How many people can play at once?**
Up to 20 per server, according to the official record.

**What is the goal?**
Per the developer: try to break all of your bones.
