"Break your Bones" is a Roblox experience built around a simple, gleeful premise: launch a character into things, watch the physics take over, and try to smash every bone in the body. ## How the game plays

Based on the advertised features, the loop is straightforward. You spawn as a ragdoll, get yourself moving, and then put that ragdoll into contact with something that will do damage: a hazard, a long fall, or both. The goal the developer states is to break all of your bones. The reward the developer states is that you unlock new ragdolls.

The "huge drop" is called out specifically in the description, which suggests it is a central set piece rather than an incidental feature. Players who want to start efficiently should probably go straight for the drop and see what the physics do, rather than hunting for a tutorial. This is a sandbox, and sandboxes reward experimentation over instruction-following.

Up to 20 players can share a server. In a physics sandbox, that matters: more players means more objects being simulated at once, more bodies being flung around, and more chances for the simulation to produce something chaotic and funny. It also means performance can vary depending on the device and the server.

## FAQ

**Is Break your Bones the same as Broken Bones IV?**
No. They are separate Roblox experiences with different creators. Do not carry assumptions about one over to the other.

**Is Break your Bones the same as the mobile "break bones" apps?**
No. Those are separate products on mobile app stores. Break your Bones is a Roblox experience created by High Effort Projects.

**Does Break your Bones have codes?**
None are documented on the official page. The description lists no code system and no redemption box. Check the official page and the developer group if you want to be certain.

**How many people can play at once?**
Up to 20 players per server, according to the official Roblox record.

**How many ragdolls are there?**
Not documented. The official description says you can "unlock new ragdolls" but does not list them or give a count.

**How do I break all my bones?**
The developer's stated objective is to break all of your bones, and the description explicitly mentions flinging yourself from a huge drop. Beyond that, the game does not publish a step-by-step method, and no article can promise a guaranteed finish — this is a physics simulation, so outcomes vary.
